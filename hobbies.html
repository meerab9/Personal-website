My Hobbies

Here are a few things I enjoy doing outside of work and school.

Bharatanatyam - Indian Classical Dance

I've been learning Indian Classical Dance for the past 12 years. I am currently a part of CU Abhinaya, the Indian Classical Dance team here at CU Boulder and have the incredible opportunity to perform at various cultural events within the community.

Spending Time Outdoors

I love spending time outdoors, especially with my family and friends. I enjoy exploring local trails and traveling around the world!

Art

I love to do art, as it is a way for me to relax and is a great creative outlet. I enjoy ceramics, painting, and drawing.
